Privacy Policy — CK9 Autofill

Last updated: October 26, 2026

1. Overview

CK9 Autofill ("the Extension") is a Chrome browser extension that helps health workers fill forms on the Ministry of Health form portal. It records form responses and re-fills them on later visits, and it can fill forms from templates included in the Extension. This Privacy Policy explains what data the Extension collects, how it is used, and how it is stored.

2. Data We Collect

The Extension collects and stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Form field values — answers you manually fill in health screening forms and choose to record (e.g., radio button selections, numeric inputs, dropdown selections). These are stored in Chrome's local storage on your device.
  • Form title — the title of the form page, used as an identifier to match the correct recording on future visits.
  • Extension settings — whether auto-fill is enabled or disabled, the last opened side panel tab, and the template list view.

The Extension does not collect:

  • Personal identity information (name, date of birth, ID number, address, etc.)
  • Browsing history or data from any other website
  • Medical records or patient data
  • Passwords or authentication credentials

Form templates

  • Templates are included in the Extension package and contain no personal data.
  • To fill a form from a template, the Extension reads only the form's questions and answer choices. It does not store the answers it fills in.
  • If the user chooses, the Extension can also press the form's submit button. The form is then sent only to the portal itself, as if the user had pressed the button.

3. How Data Is Used

Recorded data and templates are used solely to fill form fields on the portal when the user asks the Extension to. This reduces repetitive data entry for health workers and clinic staff.

4. Data Storage and Sharing

  • All data is stored locally using Chrome's chrome.storage.local API.
  • No data is transmitted to any external server, third party, or remote service.
  • No data is shared with any person or organization.
  • Data can be deleted at any time using the "Hapus Semua" button in the Extension side panel, or by uninstalling the Extension.

5. Permissions Justification

  • storage — Used to save recorded form values and extension settings locally on your device via chrome.storage.local.
  • activeTab — Allows the Extension to work with the form open in the current tab when the user uses the Extension.
  • tabs — Used to identify the active tab so the Extension's side panel can work with the form open on that page. No browsing history or data from other tabs is accessed or stored.
  • sidePanel — Used to show the Extension's interface in Chrome's side panel.

The Extension's content scripts are restricted exclusively to pages matching *://form.kemkes.go.id/*. The Extension has no access to any other website.

6. Children's Privacy

This Extension is designed for use by healthcare workers and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 years of age.
